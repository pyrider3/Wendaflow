# Temporary canvas lifetime

New canvases, including a fresh main canvas, are session drafts until the first successful manual Save. Switching tabs retains their in-memory content. Closing a draft removes its tab, catalog entry, library metadata, previews and cached placeholder; it does not move it to Trash. Reloading also drops unsaved new canvases. Saving promotes the document and enables per-canvas autosave. Imports and duplicates are explicit documents and remain available after closing.

Close operations serialize and await a pending save. Discarded ids block stale persistence and task callbacks. Closing the last draft leaves an empty picker from which a new canvas can be created. Failed metadata writes preserve the draft and report the error.

Legacy cleanup recognizes only an autogenerated New Canvas name with the exact empty cache placeholder produced by the former creation code, no saved/autosave markers, and no open tab. Nonempty, imported, copied, explicitly saved and unknown legacy data remain protected. An open recognized legacy draft gets the new temporary marker.

Validation: 104 unit tests, 6 public-policy tests, locale audit and public production build passed. Isolated browser flows verified two edited unsaved drafts (active and inactive close), picker/manager/trash/reload absence, saved content reopening, last-draft closure and recreation, legacy-placeholder cleanup with nonempty/imported/saved protection, and the full picker/management regression flow.
